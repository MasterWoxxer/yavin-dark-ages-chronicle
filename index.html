The Dark Ages Chronicle is in formation. The first pieces are being written.

Why

Meta-analyses, essays and questions about whole disciplines and fields, including the conduct of the journals themselves. The meta-analyses are not commissioned to reach a conclusion: what they will find is not known in advance.

What we publish

Essays

Arguments about what a field gets wrong, and why.

Meta-analyses

Research on research: what a body of work actually shows.

Reviews of meta-research

Fresh looks at the major studies of how science fails.

Case files

Documented institutional failures, every claim sourced.

Reviewed in the open

Pieces are reviewed and annotated on Transparent Peer Review, by reviewers whose expertise and school of thought are visible to every reader.
