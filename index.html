The Dark Ages Chronicle

How little we know, and what we're wrong about

Volume 1 · In formation
The Dark Ages Chronicle is in formation. The first pieces are being written.

Why

Meta-analyses, essays and questions about whole disciplines and fields, including the conduct of the journals themselves. Our meta-analyses are not commissioned to reach a conclusion: what they will find is not known in advance.

Where something was published confers nothing here. What a piece of work is worth depends on what it carries: data that can be repeated, logic anyone can follow, or nothing but authority.

What we publish

Meta-analyses

Research on research: what a body of work actually shows.

Reviews of meta-research

Fresh looks at the major studies of how science fails, in the line of Ioannidis.

Essays

Arguments about what a field gets wrong, and why.

Case files

Documented institutional failures, every claim sourced.

First pieces

The Chronicle opens with reviews of major existing work: the meta-research of John Ioannidis, the grievance studies affair, and research from the COVID years.

Reviewed in the open

Pieces are annotated on Transparent Peer Review, by reviewers whose expertise and standpoint are visible to every reader. Every article carries a dated corrections log.
